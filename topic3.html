Secure HTTP

HTTPS

HTTPS, which stands for Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure, uses an encryption protocol called Transport Layer Security (TLC) to keep the information that is being sent over the network secure. A lot of the information that is sent is sensitive, personal information, and it is important to keep it private. HTTPS is used by most websites today.
